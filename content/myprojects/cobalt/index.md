The Collaboration on Best Alternatives Learning and Technology (Co-BALT) aims to foster a culture of collaboration, continuous learning, and innovation. This is achieved by leveraging the collective expertise and knowledge of employees to drive process improvements that will support the organization’s strategic goals and level of efforts (LOEs).
